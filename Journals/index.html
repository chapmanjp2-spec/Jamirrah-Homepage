A Handmade Web - J.R. Carpenter

I personally felt like this story was a good explanation of how handmade websites are considered better due to the uniqueness and effort the individual put forth; handmade creativity offers higher quality than what AI could create.

As I use many websites on the daily, such as TikTok, Google, and Pinterest, I start to take in just how much time was put into those platforms for individuals like me who use them, and how these spaces were made and adapted to our liking for us to feel and find our personal comfort zones. And I love the fact that every website is diffrent to fit the personality the creator is giving it, whether it be for creating a mood board, editing videos, or creating a color palette; the layouts and settings of the website always fit.


Rant on technology - Ursula K.Le Guin

As technology has grown over the years, its advancements have also changed how we use physical materials. When you actually think about it, technology is surrounding us everywhere as we use our tablets, phones, and computers; they are now embedded with writing/ drawing apps, apps where you can customize your food, and it can be delivered or dropped off to you, and they have fridges made where it shows you what’s inside on a screen.

Just about everything we use connects with tech; I find it to be a helpful advancement, but I can’t help but worry for the future: if we continue allowing technology to do everything for us, will we slowly forget how to use necessities?


My Website is a Shifting House- Laurel Schwulst

For this reading, I enjoyed the talks about how a website can grow and/or wither throughout time; websites can hit a high with individual clicks/views, but they can also fall, which is normal. The activity of a website can slow down, but the dedication put into it will always be there.


Against an Increasingly User-Hostile Web - Parimal Satyal

This reading and our discussion kinda freaked me out, honestly. It’s like an eye-opener to see and think about how much our information is out there, being used and how we’re constantly being tracked, sometimes just by opening an app or accepting cookies. I’m sure many of us don’t think much of it, but the way people have methods to gain this info is terrifying. It’s sad to see some sites or platforms you trust to use daily do these exact methods. It really makes you think about where my info is going and why they need it.
